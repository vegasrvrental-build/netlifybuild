Questions?

Frequently Asked

What's included with the rental? ▼
Both vehicles come fully equipped: bedding, towels, kitchen utensils, plates, cookware, propane, and a starter supply of some toiletries. When you go to book, you will see a full profile and what is available.
How does the delivery service work? ▼
We deliver to most of the Southern Nevada area. If you can't tow or prefer not to drive, we can deliver and set up the RV, just let us know. Please see the pricing when booking.
What if something breaks during my rental? ▼
Message us immediately—we are not always available 24/7 but try and get back to you quickly. Most minor issues can be troubleshot over the phone. You may also opt in on third party road side assistance when booking.
What's the cancellation policy? ▼
We offer a flexible cancellation policy. Cancellation 31 days or more before start of confirmed booking = Full refund, less applicable processing fee. Cancellation 30 - 8 days before start of confirmed booking = Full refund, less applicable processing fee. Cancellation 7 days or less before start of confirmed booking = Forfeit any Booking Deposit plus applicable processing fee.
Can I take the RV out of Nevada? ▼
Yes! Both vehicles are allowed throughout the Southwest. Just let us know your route and expected return date. Arizona, Utah, and California are all common destinations.
What are the fuel/mileage costs? ▼
We send you off with full propane and fuel for the van. You pay for any fuel and / or propane consumed during your trip. Please return the van with a full tank of gas; subject to $8/gal if returned below Full. The Travato gets ~16 MPG. The van comes with 100 miles per day; anything over your allotment is charged at $0.45/mile.
